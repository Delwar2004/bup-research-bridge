# BUP Research Bridge

A centralized research collaboration platform connecting BUP students, faculty, and alumni for research opportunities, mentorship, and academic collaboration.

## 📌 Project Overview

BUP Research Bridge is a web-based platform designed to make research-related information more accessible and organized within the BUP community.

The platform aims to help students discover:

- Faculty research interests
- Research and thesis opportunities
- Previous research projects
- Alumni research experience and expertise
- Research mentorship and collaboration opportunities

Faculty and alumni will be able to manage their research profiles, share research-related information, and connect with interested students.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Centralize research-related information
- Help students find suitable research opportunities
- Improve visibility of faculty research interests
- Connect students with experienced alumni
- Support research mentorship and collaboration
- Provide searchable research and researcher profiles

## 👥 Target Users

- Students
- Faculty Members
- Alumni
- Administrators
